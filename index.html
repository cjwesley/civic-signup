Trustee Shadow Day

      
What a Shadow Day looks like

      
A Trustee Shadow Day puts you inside Village Hall for one day to see how an elected official does the work behind a Village Board meeting. It's less about watching a meeting and more about learning how decisions get shaped before anyone votes.


      
    
        
  1. 
          
    Agenda grooming at Village Hall
    
          
    Sit in on the agenda grooming process, where staff and the Trustee work through the items proposed for an upcoming Village Board meeting. You'll see how an item moves from a department request to a board packet, what gets pulled, what gets deferred, and why.
    
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          
    Evaluate the agenda like an elected official
    
          
    Work through the draft agenda from a Trustee's perspective: What is the Board actually being asked to decide? Who does this affect? What does it cost, and what is the trade-off? What questions should be asked of staff before it reaches a vote?
    
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          
    Read a board packet the way a Trustee does
    
          
    Learn how to read staff reports, ordinances, resolutions, and budget items quickly, find the parts that matter, and flag where the public record is thin.
    
        
    4. 
        
  4. 
          
    Meet the people who make Village Hall run
    
          
    Short conversations with Village staff in areas like the Clerk's office, Development Services, Finance, or Public Works, depending on what's on that week's agenda.
    
        
    5. 
        
  5. 
          
    Constituent work
    
          
    See how resident emails, calls, and requests get triaged, routed to staff, and followed up on. This is a large part of the day-to-day job that rarely makes the news.
    
        
    6. 
        
  6. 
          
    Debrief with Trustee Wesley
    
          
    Wrap up with a one-on-one conversation: what surprised you, what you'd have decided differently, and what a path into public service could look like for you.
    
        
    7. 
      


      

        
Good to know

        
    
          
  • Open to high school and college students. Younger students are welcome with a parent or guardian's consent.
    • 
          
  • Dates are scheduled around the Village Board meeting calendar, typically on a weekday.
    • 
          
  • If the day includes a public Village Board meeting, you're welcome to attend it that evening.
    • 
          
  • Dress is business casual. Bring a notebook and questions.
